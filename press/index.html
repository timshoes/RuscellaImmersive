A natural evolution

Ruscella Immersive is not a change of direction for JJ Ruscella. It is the same craft carried into a new room.

For thirty years JJ has directed the same thing in different forms: a real person, inside a story, at the moment it counts. On stage that person sat in the audience. In live-action simulation they stood in the scene. In a headset they become its protagonist. Ruscella Immersive is where those rooms meet, and where the methods he developed in each are now offered to institutions and creators.

  1. 1989–2008The stage

    Directing and producing theatre, then teaching acting, film and performance at UCF.

  2. 2001–2013The screen

    Feature film, holiday shorts and the acting program at the New York Film Academy at Disney-MGM Studios.

  3. 2002–2020The simulation

    The Interactive Performance Lab, live-action simulation for police and educators, and Shenandoah’s immersive lab.

  4. 2020 onThe headset

    The immersive design taxonomy, OrchestrateXR and federal public-safety work.

  5. TodayRuscella Immersive

    A studio that brings all four together: experiences designed in the round, produced to a director’s standard, and labs that teach others to make their own.

Where each service comes from

The peer-reviewed taxonomy JJ co-authored gives every Ri project a shared language: ten dimensions for designing around the participant, now the foundation of how the U.S. Air Force teaches XR.

Immersive Production

Immersive Labs

Ruscella Immersive is an Orlando-based immersive design and production studio led by director JJ Ruscella. It designs and produces immersive experiences, and builds the labs where institutions make their own, for clients in education, government and entertainment.

Long boilerplate

116 words

Ruscella Immersive is an Orlando-based immersive design and production studio led by JJ Ruscella, and the only immersive studio led by a stage and film director who co-authored the field’s design taxonomy. The studio works in three disciplines: Immersive Design, which shapes each experience around the participant; Immersive Production, from 360 capture and 3D development to projection mapping and live simulation; and Immersive Labs, which equip institutions to create their own. Its work includes Voices of Freedom, an immersive history series with Shenandoah University and New American History; an arts and humanities immersive lab at Christopher Newport University, built with Apple; and The Living Nativity, a live production captured in stereoscopic 360. Learn more at ruscellaimmersive.com.

About JJ Ruscella

Short biography

57 words

JJ Ruscella is a stage and film director and the co-founder and CEO of Ruscella Immersive. Over thirty years he has directed theatre, film and live-action simulation, founded the Interactive Performance Lab at the University of Central Florida, and co-authored the immersive experience design taxonomy adopted by the U.S. Air Force. He is based in Orlando, Florida.

Long biography

186 words

JJ Ruscella is a director whose work has moved from the stage to the screen to the headset. He is the co-founder and CEO of Ruscella Immersive, the Orlando studio that designs and produces immersive experiences for institutions and creators.

Trained as a theatre artist, he earned his MFA at Rutgers University’s Mason Gross School of the Arts and spent twelve years at the University of Central Florida teaching theatre and film, where he founded the Interactive Performance Lab with the School of Film and Digital Media. He directed and produced the feature film The Karaoke King, a winner at the Skyline Indie Film Festival, and received a Bronze Telly Award for directing and writing Santa Talks.

As founding executive director of the Shenandoah Center for Immersive Learning, he grew a student-run immersive design and production lab and received the Virginia Foundation for Independent Colleges’ H. Hiter Harris III Award for Excellence in Instructional Technology. He co-authored a peer-reviewed taxonomy for immersive experience design that became the foundation of the U.S. Air Force’s Vector program, and created OrchestrateXR, software for designing and orchestrating immersive learning experiences.
