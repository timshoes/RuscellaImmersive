Immersive Production

The people and processes to bring your experiences to life.

Our production team is fluent in 360 and stereoscopic capture, real-time 3D, projection mapping and spatial sound, backed by a network of crews, performers and venues from Orlando’s entertainment industry.

Core offering

Live simulation capture

We stage, direct and capture live clinical and operational simulations, then build them into immersive training that healthcare systems and government agencies run again and again.
