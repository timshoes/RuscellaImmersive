Experiences that deliver excellence.

Ruscella Immersive is the only immersive consultancy led by a stage and film director who wrote the field's design taxonomy. We serve institutions and creators who need experiences people remember.

Technology does not make an experience powerful. Direction does. Headsets change every year; what people remember does not: a moment staged with intent, a story they stand inside, and a reason to care what happens next.

From Orlando, Florida, we design, produce and build the capability to create immersive experiences for entertainment, education, government and culture.

What we believe

  • You are the hero of the story.

    Our job is to guide. The institution, the educator, the participant: they are the ones who change. We make sure they are ready.

  • Direction before technology.

    We decide what the experience must do before we decide what it runs on. The platform follows the idea, never the other way round.

  • Immersive creation belongs to everyone.

    Not just the well-resourced few. It belongs with the people who know their participants best: educators, trainers, artists and storytellers, in any discipline.

The founders

JJ Ruscella

Co-Founder · Chief Executive Officer

JJ has directed for more than thirty years: stage plays, a feature film, and live simulations for police and educators, and now immersive experiences. He founded the Interactive Performance Lab at the University of Central Florida, built the Shenandoah Center for Immersive Learning, and co-authored the taxonomy for immersive experience design used by the U.S. Air Force. He directs every Ruscella Immersive production.

Joanne Ruscella

Joanne turns bold visions into fundable, scalable, sustainable realities. Her background in operations, finance and large-scale program development means every project has someone making sure each piece ships on time, on budget and built to last.

Tim Howes

Co-Founder · Chief Creative Officer
